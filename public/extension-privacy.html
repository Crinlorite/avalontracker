Privacy Policy

Avalon Tracker Chrome Extension — by Crintech

Effective date: TBD (set on publication) Contact: claude@crintech.pro

This extension helps members of the Celtiberos Albion Online guild (and invited contributors) map the daily-randomised Avalonian Roads in Avalon Tracker.

What the extension does

When you press F9:

  1. The extension asks Chrome to capture one frame of your desktop via the standard chrome.desktopCapture API. You explicitly grant this permission on first use and can revoke it at any time via Chrome's site settings.
  2. The frame is cropped to the minimap region you previously outlined (via the in-extension calibrator) and preprocessed (grayscale + binary threshold) for OCR legibility.
  3. Tesseract.js — bundled locally — runs on the cropped image to extract text.
  4. A regex parser finds portal tooltip rows (destination + timer + capacity) and the zone-title text.
  5. The extracted fields are sent via HTTPS POST to https://avalon.crintech.pro/api/ingest/portal-snapshot with your existing avalon.crintech.pro session cookie.

Data we collect and transmit

The POST body contains only:
  • The portal rows parsed from OCR: destination name, timer hours + minutes, capacity used/max.
  • The zone-title string parsed from OCR (e.g. Teros-Auiusum) as zoneNameGuess.
  • A UTC timestamp of capture.
  • A constant source: "extension" tag.

We do NOT transmit:

  • Raw screenshots or image data.
  • Your Albion account credentials — the extension never reads Albion game files, memory, or network traffic.
  • Any data about other applications on your computer.
  • Cursor position, keyboard input, or clipboard contents.
  • Pixel data outside the calibrated minimap region.

Authentication

The extension does not store bearer tokens or passwords. Your identity on the tracker is established by your existing NextAuth session cookie on avalon.crintech.pro, which you set by signing in with Discord OAuth on the website. The extension only attaches that cookie to its own fetch() calls — it does not read, copy, or persist the cookie itself.

Permissions explained

  • desktopCapture — required to capture the game screen when you press F9. One-shot, user-consented, no continuous streaming.
  • offscreen — hosts the image-processing DOM context (canvas + Tesseract) in a background page, since the service worker has no DOM.
  • storage — persists two things locally in chrome.storage.local: the minimap region rectangle (from the calibrator) and the offline-retry queue of failed POSTs (drained on reconnection).
  • notifications — surfaces capture results and errors via Chrome's notification system.
  • alarms — wakes the service worker every 5 minutes to drain the offline queue when the tracker was temporarily down.
  • host_permissions: https://avalon.crintech.pro/* — restricts the extension's network access to exactly one domain.

Third-party services

  • Avalon Tracker (avalon.crintech.pro) — the parsed OCR fields are sent here for use in the route graph. Data retention and handling are governed by the tracker's own privacy policy.
  • Tesseract.js — OCR engine bundled into the extension. Runs locally in your browser; makes no network calls.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, fingerprinting, or advertising SDKs.

Data retention

Locally in your browser

  • Minimap region rectangle: kept until you recalibrate or uninstall.
  • Offline queue entries: kept until successfully delivered or until the 100-item cap overflows (oldest dropped).
  • Last capture metadata (portal count + timestamp): overwritten on every capture.

Uninstalling the extension clears all locally stored data.

On the Avalon Tracker server

Your parsed portal snapshots are associated with your Discord-linked user account on the tracker. You can request deletion by contacting the developer or revoking your access in the tracker admin panel.

Security

  • All network traffic is HTTPS only (enforced by host_permissions).
  • We do not load remote scripts. All JavaScript, WASM, and assets are bundled into the extension package (MV3 CSP requirement).
  • The extension is not open-source at this time (private GitHub repository); source disclosure is available on request for security audit.

Changes to this policy

If the extension adds new data-collection behaviour, this policy will be updated and the extension version bumped. Material changes will be announced on the tracker home page and via an in-extension notification.
